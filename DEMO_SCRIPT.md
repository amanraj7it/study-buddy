# 🎯 60-Second Judge Demo Walkthrough Script
### Education Chest — Solving The Learning Gap After School

Use this quick walkthrough to present Education Chest to hackathon judges for maximum impact.

---

### ⏱️ Minute 0:00 - The Hook & Problem Statement
1. **Navigate to**: `http://localhost:5173/pitch-analytics`
2. **Say to Judges**:
   > *"Judges, millions of students fall behind every single year because private coaching costs ₹2,000 to ₹4,000 per month, and their working parents cannot help them with advanced homework. Education Chest bridges this gap by replacing expensive tuition with empathetic AI tutoring, peer study circles, and multilingual parent reports."*
3. **Show**:
   - Highlight the live impact metrics: **1,240+ students helped**, **₹18.5+ Lakhs saved**, **4,890 doubts solved**.
   - Point out the 5 core solution pillars.

---

### ⏱️ Minute 0:20 - AI Doubt Solver & Doubt Journal
1. **Navigate to**: `http://localhost:5173/doubts` (or click "Ask a Doubt" in the sidebar)
2. **Click**: Any one-click sample question button (e.g. *📐 Solve: 3x + 5 = 20* or *🌌 Why is the sky blue?*).
3. **Click**: **"Solve My Doubt ✨"**
4. **Show**:
   - The 4-step pedagogical breakdown: Given & Goal, Core Principle, Step-by-Step Working, Verification.
   - The **"Why This Works"** intuition box.
   - The **Active Recall Practice Problem** (encouraging self-testing, not cheating).
5. **Switch Tab**: Click **"Doubt Journal"** tab.
   - Show the saved revision cards with **"Needs Revision"** and **"Mastered"** toggle chips.

---

### ⏱️ Minute 0:35 - Peer Study Circles & Gamified Helpfulness
1. **Navigate to**: `http://localhost:5173/circles` (or click "Study Circles" in sidebar)
2. **Show**:
   - Grade-filtered study circles (Grade 8 to College).
3. **Click**: **"Class 10 CBSE Board Prep"** circle.
4. **Demonstrate**:
   - Group chat: Type a quick message ("Can anyone help with physics?").
   - Click **"Shared Doubts (1)"** tab: Show the homework doubt posted by classmate Rahul.
   - Click the **Upvote (👍 8)** button on Priya's answer: Notice how Priya's reputation score increases (+15 pts) and the toast confirms gamified reward!
   - Click **"Leaderboard"** tab: Show top peer tutors ranked by community helpfulness points.

---

### ⏱️ Minute 0:50 - Smart Adaptive Planner & Weak Topics
1. **Navigate to**: `http://localhost:5173/smart-planner`
2. **Show**:
   - Point out the banner: *"Intervention topics automatically diagnosed from your Doubt Journal"* (e.g., Trigonometry, Quadratic Factoring).
3. **Click**: **"Generate Adaptive Plan ⚡"**
4. **Click**: **"Push Plan to My Timetable (1-Click)"**
   - Confetti burst fires!
   - Sessions are saved into the student's study timetable.

---

### ⏱️ Minute 1:00 - Parent Dashboard & Vernacular WhatsApp Sharing
1. **Navigate to**: `http://localhost:5173/parent-report`
2. **Show**:
   - Clear, positive headline: *"Completed 9.5 hours of focused study this week! 🌟"*
   - Switch language button to **"हिंदी (Hindi)"** or **"मराठी (Marathi)"**: Show the report instantly translating into the parent's mother tongue.
3. **Click**: **"Send via WhatsApp"** or **"Copy SMS / WhatsApp Text"**:
   - Shows the clean, formatted message ready to send to working parents without academic jargon.

---

### ⏱️ Closing Summary
> *"Education Chest makes after-school academic guidance 100% free, community-powered, and parent-accessible. Thank you!"*
